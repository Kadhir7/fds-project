04 · SALES FORECAST

Predict Brand Sales in India

Yearly smartphone shipments of each brand in India, with a trend forecast for the coming years.

REAL DATA Brand-level India shipments (million units) taken from Canalys, Omdia and IDC press releases. Model-wise, state-wise and city-wise unit sales are only sold in paid trackers, so they are not included.

Select a brand, then press Forecast.

Reality check for 2026: the trend line cannot see sudden shocks. IDC expects India's 2026 shipments to fall to roughly 128–130 million (from about 152 million in 2025) and Counterpoint expects a 13% fall, because memory chip prices have shot up. Treat the 2026 forecast as optimistic.

Dataset used (million units)

Sources

  • 2022: Canalys via GSMArena – full-year 2022 report
  • 2023: Canalys India Q4 2023 / full year
  • 2024: Canalys India Q4 2024 / full year
  • 2025: Omdia – brand figures and press release
  • Apple: IDC – 2024 and 2025
  • 2026 outlook: IDC and Counterpoint projections

Definitions differ slightly between firms (vivo excludes iQOO, Xiaomi includes POCO, OnePlus is counted inside OPPO), so compare brands within one year rather than treating the series as one exact dataset. OnePlus has no separate published unit figure, and OPPO's definition changed between years, so neither is included.
